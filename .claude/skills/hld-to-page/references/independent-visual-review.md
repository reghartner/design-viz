# Independent visual review

Use this optional route only when delegation is available and already
authorized, and when actual rendered captures can be produced without making
an unrepaired candidate the user's first preview. It does not grant permission
to launch another agent, contact anyone, or expand the requested deliverables.
The author does not need browser access: a coordinator or other authorized
capture provider may supply the actual screenshots. If actual captures are
unavailable, or no authorized independent critic is available, finish the
normal self-audit and report the visual checks that remain unverified.

## Review packet

Give the critic only what is needed to judge the candidate:

- actual rendered captures for every authored view at the intended host width,
  covering the focal state and enough steps/path endings to see the promised
  changes;
- the source material needed to check the depicted claims;
- the operator's current answers; and
- the filled per-view visual brief.

Do not include benchmark rubrics, hidden answers, other graders' findings or
scores, or private reasoning. In connected workflows, keep capture and review
artifacts outside the candidate spec/ledger source pair. When an evaluation
requires the first draft as evidence, preserve it before repair rather than
overwriting it.

## One bounded pass

Ask for one prioritized list of concrete, screenshot-grounded findings. Each
finding names the view and state shown, the visible problem, and the smallest
useful repair. The critic checks whether focal evidence and promised states are
visible, labels are legible and uncropped, panels and controls obstruct the
graph, density supports the planned reading order, and illustrative staging
implies unsupported behavior or meaningful physical placement.

The critic reports findings; it does not author the spec, invent a mechanism,
change the source, decide factual ambiguity, or approve the candidate. Apply
one scoped repair pass, then revalidate, re-run the affected worksheet audit,
and recapture the affected views/states. Stop there and submit the normal paired
spec and ledger for human preview and approval. The critic never accepts on the
user's behalf, and this route does not create an indefinite review loop.
