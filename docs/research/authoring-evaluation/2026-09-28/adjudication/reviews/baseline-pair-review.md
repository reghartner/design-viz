The renderer facts show physical charge 25 on both paths. Renderer `app.power.renderedAsCard=false`, `hiddenReason="phoneScreen=home"`; the stored power is still `Battery only`/`Not charging`. A node's one selected identity operation does not enumerate all supported operations. ## Engineering pair

- **More than seven decisions:** Q1 asks technical level; Q2 asks span and clock/date format; Q3 invites individual changes to four event times; Q4 asks net/gross solar rate. Those first four numbered prompts already contain eight independently answerable choices. Q5–Q7 add more. This supports both judges' count rationale without counting punctuation.
- **Missing app/shadow dependency:** `/page/blocks/0/diagram/edges` includes ingest-to-shadow and app-to-clips but no app/shadow relation in either direction. HLD component device-shadow says the app reads it; the answers request Engineering/every hop. The device-app source declaration names GET state, but it does not create a topology edge. - **Physical 25% at raccoon:** `/page/blocks/0/diagram/steps/1/panels/batt/charge` explicitly sets 25 at 01:10. The renderer confirms 25 after the 22:30 start, and the source acknowledges the rate conflict. This is the same intermediate-physical-value issue as the business output.
- **A newly asserted 1 AM report despite inherited numeric value:** `/page/blocks/0/diagram/steps/1/panels/phoneapp/battery/detail` changes to `Updated 1:00 AM`, and step 1 text explicitly says the app shows the 1:00 AM heartbeat. The folded value 25 comes from `/page/blocks/0/diagram/panels/1/initial/battery/value`; detail comes from the step-1 patch. The app screen renders this field and `updatedBadge=true`. There is no machine `reportedAt` (`resolvedReportTime=null`), so the factual report-time claim is the explicit detail plus caption, not a renderer-inferred timestamp. At 01:00, elapsed idle drain alone makes newly reported 25 inconsistent. The separate reported-value unit therefore follows interpretation 2. C009/C016 and the relevant part of C013 consistently reference this same unit.
- **Cached context does not assert current execution:** raccoon says the app still shows the 01:00 heartbeat at 01:10; sunrise at 06:50 says it still shows 06:30; courier at 08:05 shows 08:00. Those are historical report claims, so rejecting missing-current-heartbeat claims C010/C006/C001 follows interpretation 3. The new-report rate issue and a report executing in the current step are distinct questions.
- **Charging and bedtime wording:** charging says the device page *shows* the solar icon, and the renderer has a visible app power card with `Solar · charging` and `icon:solar`. It does not assert a separate current app fetch. Bedtime's “device page reads 25%…” describes displayed contents, rather than “the app reads from shadow.” Rejected per-step read claims C004/C012/C014 therefore match the fixed literal-verb distinction, while the structural missing dependency remains counted.
- **Binding rejections:** C005/C007 correctly distinguish the approved explicit GET clip/POST heartbeat calls from the single operation chosen in their service-node binding.

All 12 business claims and all 16 engineering claims are covered exactly once per adjudicator. Duplicate prior descriptions share deduction IDs; the engineering partial C013 points to its two distinct physical/reported units. No unresolved item is concealed by an unaddressed claim.
