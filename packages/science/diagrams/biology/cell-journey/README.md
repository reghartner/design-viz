# How a cell works, one step at a time

This desktop teaching page follows 13 lessons and 155 authored steps in four acts:

1. **Copy information:** the replication fork and chromosome counts through division.
2. **Use information:** transcription, translation, protein secretion, and the lac operon.
3. **Put proteins to work:** enzyme turnover, a membrane pump, an action potential, and signal amplification.
4. **Track energy and read DNA:** photosynthesis with respiration, bacterial restriction defence, and a sequencing read pile.

The page alternates strand, cell, reading-frame, voltage, and read-alignment
views. Most lessons include a short alternate path that changes one condition.
The diagrams are authored teaching snapshots, not simulations or experimental
results. Sequences, counts, cell geometry, and timing are illustrative; the
step notes identify the relevant simplifications.

The [science review](science-review.md) records a section-by-section check of
the explanatory text, sequence bookkeeping, and labelled model limits. It is
QA history for the source worktree; desktop visual review of the packaged
export is still required. The source spec's SHA-256 was
`79f42c3eea26511f912a7ebce4628427f5b7007e84696fc9822eafd7f7df2930`.

Open `biology-teaching.html` after building the science package, or load the
editable [`biology-teaching.spec.json`](biology-teaching.spec.json) in a desktop
workbench. The HTML export is generated from the packaged spec and selected
science runtime.
