# Season of seats

The [authored story](season-of-seats.spec.json) follows a hockey club's season from a player-driven demand surge through schedule planning, ticket sales, game-night fulfillment, follow-up, and renewal. Its 14 chapters contain 173 steps. The recurring UBS Arena seat map lets a reader see holds, sell-through, pricing, and audience segments on the same venue as the story advances.

Public New York Islanders dates, promotions, and cited figures appear with source links at the relevant steps. The ticketing, CRM, app, scanning, and point-of-sale workflows are illustrative reconstructions, not claims about the club's or a vendor's systems. Unless a step cites a source for a figure, capacities by section, section sales, prices, forecasts, conversion rates, and operational counts are illustrative. See [the source review](source-review.md) for the fact-check record and its remaining source limitations.

The story moves through four teaching arcs:

1. **Create demand:** the Schaefer effect, the schedule drop, a bobblehead production clock, and group holds.
2. **Sell the inventory:** on-sale day, demand-based pricing, heritage-night links, and the difference between an offer for the first 10,000 fans and one for everyone.
3. **Serve and learn from a game night:** ticket ownership versus attendance, gate stock, a score-first sponsor offer, and the morning-after recap.
4. **Build the next season:** the path from a friend's ticket to membership, then the clinch-or-fade renewal fork.

The canonical source was rolled up from the stranded `showcase-content/showcase/sports/sports-marketing.spec.json` workspace. The production story is kept in this optional package so a company or site that has not enabled `sports` does not load it. Author and edit it on desktop.
